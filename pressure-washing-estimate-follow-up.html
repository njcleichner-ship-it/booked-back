Pressure washing estimate follow-up: a respectful 14-day process

Pressure-washing quotes can vary by surface, square footage, staining, access, water availability, nearby plants, and whether soft washing or another method is appropriate. Helpful follow-up clarifies those real details rather than repeatedly asking whether the customer is ready to book.

Suggested schedule

  1. Day 0: Send the estimate and summarize the included surfaces, treatment assumptions, access needs, and exclusions.
  2. Day 2: Confirm receipt and offer to clarify one scope question.
  3. Day 5: Add a useful detail about preparation, plant protection, water access, expected finish, or weather-dependent timing.
  4. Day 9: Ask whether the project is still being considered and whether the surfaces or timing changed.
  5. Day 14: Close the loop and pause unless the customer engages.
Receipt-check example

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Pressure Washing Business]. I’m checking that the estimate for [specific surfaces] reached you. I’m happy to clarify the included areas or preparation details if helpful.

Close-the-loop example

Hi [Name], I’ll pause my follow-ups on the [project] estimate so I don’t crowd your messages. If you want to revisit the surfaces or timing later, reply here anytime. Thanks for considering [Pressure Washing Business].

What to record

Track the customer, property area or surfaces, estimate date, last contact, next action, next-action date, and outcome. Keep addresses and other private information only in tools appropriate for your business.

Keep every message accurate

  • Do not invent limited availability, weather urgency, or price deadlines.
  • Refer only to property conditions and surfaces you actually observed or discussed.
  • Honor requested timing, firm decisions, and opt-outs immediately.
  • Review every message before sending.
