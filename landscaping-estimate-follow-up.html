Landscaping estimate follow-up: a respectful 14-day process

Landscaping estimates can depend on plant choices, site access, drainage, materials, seasonality, and weather. Useful follow-up clarifies genuine project details instead of repeatedly asking for a decision.

Suggested schedule

  1. Day 0: Send the estimate and summarize the included work, materials, assumptions, and exclusions.
  2. Day 2: Confirm receipt and offer to clarify the scope.
  3. Day 5: Add one relevant detail about plant options, preparation, access, drainage, material quantities, or realistic timing.
  4. Day 9: Ask whether the project is still under consideration or whether priorities have changed.
  5. Day 14: Close the loop politely and pause unless the customer engages.
Receipt-check example

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Landscaping Business]. I’m checking that the estimate for [specific project area] reached you. I’m happy to clarify the materials, preparation, or timing assumptions if helpful.

Close-the-loop example

Hi [Name], I’ll pause my follow-ups on the [project] estimate so I don’t crowd your messages. If you want to revisit the scope or timing later, reply here anytime. Thanks for considering [Landscaping Business].

Keep every message accurate

  • Do not invent material shortages, weather urgency, or scheduling deadlines.
  • Use only true project-specific details.
  • Honor requested timing, firm decisions, and opt-outs immediately.
  • Review each message before sending.
