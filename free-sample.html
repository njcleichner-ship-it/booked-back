The 3-Message Estimate Follow-Up Mini Kit

A practical starting point for home-service contractors who want to stay consistent without sounding pushy.

1. Confirm it arrived

Send about two days after the estimate unless the customer gave another timeframe.

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Business]. Just checking that the estimate for [specific project] reached you. No rush—I’m happy to clarify anything about [relevant scope detail].

2. Add useful context

Send only when you have a truthful, project-specific detail to add.

Hi [Name], one detail that may help as you review the [project] estimate: [brief explanation of scope, material, preparation, timing, or options]. Would you like me to explain either option?

3. Close the loop

Use after prior follow-ups received no reply.

Hi [Name], I’ll pause my follow-ups on the [project] estimate so I don’t crowd your messages. If you want to revisit it later, reply here anytime. Thanks for considering [Business].

Before sending

  • Replace every bracketed field.
  • Mention one real project detail.
  • Make only accurate claims.
  • Use an appropriate channel and identify the business.
  • Stop after an opt-out or firm no.
  • Follow applicable laws.
