Cleaning estimate follow-up: a respectful 14-day process

Cleaning quotes often depend on home size, current condition, visit frequency, pets, priority rooms, and whether the customer needs a standard, deep, or move-related cleaning. Helpful follow-up clarifies those details instead of repeatedly asking whether the customer is ready to book.

Suggested schedule

  1. Day 0: Send the quote and summarize the cleaning type, included areas, frequency, and exclusions.
  2. Day 2: Confirm receipt and offer to clarify one scope question.
  3. Day 5: Add a useful detail about supplies, pets, access, add-ons, or what the customer should expect on the first visit.
  4. Day 9: Ask whether the cleaning is still being considered and whether the scope or timing changed.
  5. Day 14: Close the loop and pause unless the customer engages.
Receipt-check example

Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from [Cleaning Business]. I’m checking that the quote for [cleaning type or property] reached you. I’m happy to clarify what is included or adjust the scope if your priorities changed.

Close-the-loop example

Hi [Name], I’ll pause my follow-ups on the [cleaning type] quote so I don’t crowd your messages. If you want to revisit the scope or timing later, reply here anytime. Thanks for considering [Cleaning Business].

What to record

Track the customer, property or cleaning type, quote date, last contact, next action, next-action date, and outcome. Keep addresses, access details, and other private information only in tools appropriate for your business.

Keep every message accurate

  • Do not invent limited availability or price deadlines.
  • Use property-specific details only when they are true and necessary.
  • Honor requested timing, firm decisions, and opt-outs immediately.
  • Review every message before sending.
